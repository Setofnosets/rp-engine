# Neo Terra Districts

## Downtown

Neo Terra's corporate core: towers, plazas, and the institutions everyone pretends are independent.

- Regions: Center, HighriseDistrictA, HighriseDistrictB, HighriseDistrictC

- Tags: corporate, civic, high-value

## Harbor

Container yards, docks, islands, and the unlicensed shipping that keeps Clarity's black market alive.

- Regions: MajorDockA, MajorDockB, DockC

- Tags: docks, smuggling, waterfront

## Maple Heights

Tree-lined residential blocks and the Matic estate. Civilian ground that villains keep making personal.

- Regions: ResidentialDistrictA, ResidentialDistrictB, ResidentialDistrictC, ResidentialDistrictD, UpperMiddleResidentialA, ParkA, ParkB

- Tags: residential, civilian, matic-family

## Fashion Row

Boutiques, ateliers, and Monicium showrooms. Superhero couture by day, image warfare by night.

- Regions: HighriseDistrictD, HighriseDistrictE

- Tags: fashion, commerce, superhero-support

## Civic Center

Police headquarters, hospitals, and the administrative machinery that tries to keep metahuman incidents legible. No dedicated SVG polygon; its facilities are grouped from key locations.

- Tags: civic, police, hospital

## Industrial Belt

Dead factories, salvage crews, warehouses, and the kind of quiet that hides laboratories and bodies.

- Regions: IndustrialA, IndustrialB, WarehousesA, SlumA, SlumB

- Tags: industrial, salvage, hideout

## Coast

Beaches, islands, and Sand Shine Town. The city's edge, where storms and old grief wash ashore.

- Regions: IslandA, IslandB, IslandC, River, Bay

- Tags: coastal, waterfront, menis-family

## Old Hills

Old money, lake parks, forested ridges, and the Renya shrine hidden where the city forgets to look.

- Regions: HighClassResidentialA, HighClassResidentialB, HighClassResidentialC, HighClassResidentialD, LakePark, ParkC, ParkD, UndevelopedForestMountains

- Tags: old-money, parks, renya-clan

## Outskirts

Airports, containment facilities, and the long roads out of Neo Terra. Officially outside the action; never actually safe.

- Regions: Airport, SmallAirport

- Tags: airport, prison, edge

## Underground

Sealed subway tunnels and unlisted facilities beneath the city. No polygon; travel is authored through secret edges.

- Tags: tunnels, stealth, virtual
