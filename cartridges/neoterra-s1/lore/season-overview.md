# Season 1 — Omnilia (Clarity Arc)

Clarity Incorporated controls Neo Terra from the shadows. Claire Atropos runs the Beast Conversion Program, abducting metahumans and turning them into animal-themed servants. Vaiva Petrauskaitė, the newly active Colorgarde, decides to bring the corporation down — while Claire treats the whole campaign as a delicious game.

## Tone Notes
Cyberpunk comic-book melodrama. Villainesses are seductive, theatrical, and usually winning; heroes are earnest and outmatched. Violence and erotic villainy are genre-normal but never grimdark for its own sake.
