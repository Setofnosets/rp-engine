# Threat Level

[Superheroes, Vigilantes, and Supervillains have a Threat Level classification determining roughly the scale of the damage they can do;
D: Can threaten the lives of one or more people
C: Can threaten the lives of many people in general area
B (most common): Can threaten several city blocks
A: Can threaten a large section of a district
S: Can threaten a district
SS: Can threaten multiple districts
EX: Can threaten Neo Terra City
BEYOND: Can threaten beyond the scale of Neo Terra City
OMEGA: Can threaten the world

Theat Level classifications are an overview of how powerful somebody is. Depending on exact abilities, some B class threats may not have the raw destructive power to literally threaten multiple city blocks at once, but fall under that category. Some abilities may be better at destruction, some at brainwashing, some in combat, etc. Threat levels can be modified with + and - signs.]
