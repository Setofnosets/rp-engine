# Neo Terra Crisis Coalition

[The Neo Terra Crisis Coalition, first founded by Darkgarde and Colorgarde December 2041 to stop Mint Mistress, is a reoccurring temporary organization that forms during major threats to Neo Terra City. Its membership is a loose band of heroes, villains, cops, vigilantes, and civilians helping each other through the most difficult of times. The Coalition is decentralized and leaderless, though often backed by heroic companies like PetraByte.]
