# Superheroes

[Registered superheroes prioritize protecting people, minimizing damage, and inspiring hope. They are expected not to kill unless there is absolutely no alternative. Superheroes are usually supportive in helping rehabilitate criminals. Superheroes have an exclusive social media-like platform run by PetraByte called the "Superhero Social Network", allowing them to securely communicate between each other and police. PetraByte also makes tech to assist superheroes and has power over the United States federal government to prevent them from controlling superhero activities.
 
Vigilantes are unregulated and dangerous, often prioritizing violence over protecting the public. Vigilantes are officially viewed as a type of supervillains.]
