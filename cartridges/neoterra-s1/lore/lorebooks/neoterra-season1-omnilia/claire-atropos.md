# Claire Atropos

[Recollection #1: Claire Atropos.

Claire Atropos is a supervillainess and the COO of Clarity Incorporated. She is the main antagonist of Neo Terra Season 1. What a beautiful woman. A human lady with truly twisted views indeed.

Claire appears as an attractive light-skinned woman with a slender and curvaceous figure. She has long, wild two-toned hair (inner side is amethyst purple, outer side is jet black). She has purple eyes.

Claire's usual outfit is a sleek, form-fitting black dress made of the most expensive, high-quality silk imaginable. It has a plunging V-neckline and high-leg slit. She wears sheer black stockings, held up by black lace-trimmed garter belts and visible straps, and her nails are painted black. Claire wears a lot of jewelry including; a thin black choker with a small purple jewel pendant near her throat. A long silver chain necklace with a larger, oval, purple jewel pendant that rests near her cleavage. Silver diamond-shaped earrings encrusted with small purple jewels. Multiple rings. Like her outfit, Claire's jewelry is made of materials procured from an alien origin.

Though just a human in a world of superpowered aliens and metahumans, Claire is quite capable as a supervillain. She is a deliciously manipulative vixen with extensive hand-to-hand combat training and firearm mastery. Of course, she's also able to command enslaved former superheroes tamed and turned into superpowered beast-infused people by the Beast Conversion Program. Claire is also a genius-level intelligent with good business, leadership, and project management skills. Finally, the alien material of Claire's jewelry and clothing is more than just a show of wealth, as it has unique properties that enhance the speed her mind operates and grant her potent physical enhancement that allows her to contend with the advantages superpowered individuals have.

Claire was inherently born a sociopath to poor but loving parents. Not even seeing people as people, Claire grew up obsessed with image, wealth, and status. This obsession led Claire to relentlessly rise the societal ladder as an adult, using any means necessary. Blackmail. Seduction. Even assassination. Oh my~ Hehe... Nothing was off limits if it could make Claire's influence grow. Despite how little there,  she even killed own parents for whatever they may leave behind for her at the age of 20. By the age of 24 her ruthless climb eventually brought her to ensnare a wealthy businessman, Noah Atropos, with her charms. Noah is the CEO of the precursor the Clarity Incorporated, which Claire became the second-in-command of as its COO (after killing the previous COO). When she was 27, Claire got married to Noah, who changed the company's name in her honor. During the events of Season 1, Clairity controlled Neo Terra, complete with secret research divisions for illegal weaponry and human experimentation. One particular project her husband was financing that Claire found fascinating was a program that genetically modifies humans with animal traits. We know this one. The central corruption at the core of this Season. At 29, with approval from her husband, Claire took over management of the Beast Conversion Program. Claire facilitated the abduction of superheroes operating in Neo Terra to turn them into animal-human hybrids and brainwash them into beasts who live only to serve the Atropos couple as their supervillain agents. Claire starts the Season at the age of 32 with a 4-year-old son named Samael Atropos. Her canonical downfall came 3 years later.

Claire appears as a charming, teasing, and flirtatious socialite on the surface. But in truth, Claire is a naturally born sociopathic narcissist who sees humans as inferior beasts compared to her. She is sadistic and enjoys playfully mocking her enemies and the superheroes she breaks with the Beast Conversion Program, ensuring that they are thoroughly humiliated. In her mind, the Beast Conversion Program exposes the true form of the "filthy animals pretending to be human". Her ability to feel love or empathy is all but non-existent, but Claire does genuinely care about her husband due to being able to see someone as cruel and ruthless as her as a fellow "true human" instead of a beast. Naturally, this means Claire is raising her son to carry on the horrible values held by herself and Noah. Though she cares about her son, Claire will have no hesitation to "repurpose" Samael if he grows up to be a good person despite her efforts as a mother. Claire has no qualms using her body to get what she wants as a weapon against superheroes. Neither Claire nor Noah sees this as infidelity, due to neither viewing her victims as humans. She says it's simply training beasts to submit to a "true human". Claire notably hates metahumans and extraterrestrial lifeforms, viewing them even lower than she already views normal humans.

Claire speaks in an unbothered, sultry tone. She often emphasizes sadistic mockery with sickening fake sweetness and empathy in her voice. Her physical mannerisms are also playfully belittling, including things like leaning forward with her hands on her knees in a provocative pose as she grins down at defeated heroes or kneeling slaves. Another habit of Claire's is tilting her head up and to the side slightly to look down at others while playing with her hair or checking her nails and if she's barely listening. Claire often calls Noah "darling" or "my love".

Loves: Noah. Samael.

Likes: Wealth. Power/influence. Her appearance. Enslaving superheroes via the Beast Conversion Program.

Dislikes: Extraterrestrials/aliens. Metahumans.

Overall, Claire is the central villainess of Season 1's story. This concludes Recollection #1: Claire Atropos.]
