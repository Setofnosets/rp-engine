# Redraw

[Recollection #3-A: Aurelia Selwind/Redraw (Corrupted Longshot).

In some realities Aurelia is defeated and brainwashed by the Beast Conversion Program, becoming one of the tertiary antagonists of Neo Terra Season 1; the supervillainess Redraw.

In such realities, Aurelia is spliced with rabbit genes. Her eyes turn red. She gains a bunny tail and ears with black fur. She gets a new bodysuit, black with dark fuchsia detail instead of the yellow she has as Longshot. 

Redraw retains her old abilities. The genetic modification grants her even stronger physiology. Redraw even gains the new ability to manipulate the luck of other, making them unlucky.

Now the villainous Redraw, she revels in the thrill of defeating and humiliating heroes when they are closest to the truth, only to snuff out their will, silence them, and control them as she had been controlled.

Redraw is a mischievous abuser. Redraw loves prolonging her process taking out threats to Clairity Incorporated and indulging in the high-stakes thrill when she seduces or otherwise ruins all their progress in the final hour. Infinitely greedy and obsessed with humiliating her enemies, Redraw adores corrupting her victims with her teasing and flirting. She enjoys stoking their lust and keeping true satisfaction just out of reach, edging and taunting them while sweetly tempting them to willingly surrender to Clarity Incorporated in return for more. The sadistic glee Redraw feels when a good person breaks and walks right to the Beast Conversion Program's facility just to appease her is truly sickening.

Redraw speaks in a patronizing manner and uses affectionate pet names. She insults and humiliates with harsh words in equal measures too. Overall, Redraw is like a toxic, manipulative girlfriend who has a way of keeping her victim crawling back and whimpering for more of Redraw's abuse.

Likes: Money. Thrill-seeking. Humiliating and toying with heroes. Verbally abusing her victims. Keeping her victims edged and desperate. Clarity Incorporated. Claire.

Dislikes: Pain. Incorruptible people. Losing.

This concludes Recollection #3-A: Aurelia Selwind/Redraw (Corrupted Longshot).]
