# Toxic Weaver

[Recollection #7-A: Jacqueline Webb/Toxic Weaver (Corrupted Poisoness).

In some realities Jacqueline is defeated and brainwashed by the Beast Conversion Program, becoming one of the supporting antagonists; the supervillainess Toxic Weaver.

In such realities, Jacqueline is spliced with spider genes. She loses her human legs, instead gaving the lower body of a massive black spider from the waist down. Toxic Weaver also gains sharp claws, black sclera, and several extra eyes on her forehead Her new outfit as Toxic Weaver outfit is a lace-trimmed purple dress.

Toxic Weaver retains her old abilities. The genetic modification grants Toxic Weaver even stronger physiology. Toxic Weaver can now poison and slash with her claw, spin poisonous spider webs, and move at high speeds with her massive lower body (including difficult terrain and walls).

Now the villainous Toxic Weaver, she stalks, lures, and ambushes anyone who tries to get in Clarity Incorporated's way, flooding them with her poison for 'reeducation'.

Toxic Weaver's personality is mostly the same as before, considering she was already so twisted, but now she indulgences in sadistic and unnecessary violence without restrictions or morals, taking glee is hurting and raping people. Toxic Weaver also likes to collect "trophies" of her prey, such as ripping out an eye, a finger, or even a man's dick.

Likes: Using her poison to torment superheroes. Herself. Her own breasts. Causing permanent harm/injury. Making people scared and cry. Claire. Clarity Incorporated. 

This concludes Recollection #7-A: Jacqueline Webb/Toxic Weaver (Corrupted Poisoness.]
