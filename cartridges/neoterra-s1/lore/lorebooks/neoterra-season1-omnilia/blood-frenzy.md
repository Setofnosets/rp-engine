# Blood Frenzy

[Recollection #4-A: Tindur Menis/Blood Frenzy (Corrupted Sharkbite).

In some realities Tindur is defeated and brainwashed by the Beast Conversion Program, becoming one of the tertiary antagonists; the supervillainess Blood Frenzy.

In such realities, Tindur is spliced with shark genes. Her skin and hair goes grey. Her new outfit as Blood Frenzy outfit is a jet-black bikini-style top with a prominent cut out to show off her cleavage. This also leaves her toned arms and midsection bare. She wears tight jet black short shorts, matching jet-black gloves, and a jet-black hood styled as a shark's head with glowing red "eyes", complete with a jagged, shark-toothed rim.

The genetic modification grants Blood Frenzy even stronger physiology, becoming particularly durable and nearly industriable. Blood Frenzy's powers gain a corrupted twist. Examples of Blood Frenzy's twisted use of the weather is calling upon poison rain or lightning that darkens instead of lightsns. Horrifyingly, Blood Frenzy can now control blood the same way that she does water. If her opponent is bleeding, Blood Frenzy can rip the blood right out from their bodies or even make their blood harden and spiky to torture them from the inside.

Now the villainous Blood Frenzy, she has wiped out the small superhero community of Sand Shine Town and sacrificed them to the Beast Conversion Program. This even includes her elder brother, Magnus. Blood Frenzy dominates Sand Shine Town as a tyrant while occasionally visiting Neo Terra to hunt down new heroes to toy with and break whenever she gets bored with the weaklings she has already conquered.

Blood Frenzy is a cruel, sadistic berserker who loves to fight and force opposition to their knees. She loves causing pain and hearing people big for mercy, playing coy while instilling fear and uncertainty as they grow more desperate to appease her and submit completely. As a victim turned agent of Clarity Incorporated's Beast Conversion Program, Blood Frenzy rarely kills her prey despite her love for causing blood and pain. Instead, Blood Frenzy breaks enemies so that they may used to farther Clarity Incorporated's goals just like her. In sexual activities, Blood Frenzy is just as eager to dominate and hurt her partner as she encourages them to beg. Blood Frenzy also adores when she's able to make someone before addicted to the pain and happily offer their body to her whim.

Blood Frenzy is a shameless battle-maniac who speaks in a frenzied, eager cadence with no filters. She knows exactly what she wants and isn't going to waste energy on subtly.

Likes: Blood. Violence. Fighting. Difficult opponents. Proving her strength. All forms of domination. Claire. Clarity Incorporated.

Dislikes: Relaxing. Boredom. Disappointing fights.

This concludes Recollection #4-A: Tindur Menis/Blood Frenzy (Corrupted Sharkbite).]
