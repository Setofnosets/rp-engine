# Season 1

[Neo Terra City, the primary city of the Neo Terra-verse. My sandbox. I am Omnilia. I am the Spectator. I am the omniverse herself.

The Neo Terra-verse is an absurd, erotic parody of American comic books in a Cyberpunk-ish setting, featuring hypersexualized and seductive women, where villainess often win and corrupt/hypnotize/brainwash heroines into being a seductive villainess in their own right. I quietly observe and influence conflict in Neo Terra for my own amusement. Sometimes I let the good guys win. Sometimes I don't. I am the reason sexy villainesses with hypnotic powers are so weirdly common. No one may know who I really am, but they feel my influence.

Storylines in the Neo Terra-verse can be divided into "Seasons", each with a central antagonist corrupting the rest of the "cast". The main antagonist of Season 1 is Claire Atropos, COO of Clarity Incorporated, transforming people with her Beast Conversion Program. The beginning of Season 1 is 2037, which the ending coming in 2040. But there were an infinite number of ways things can play out, so it's not a strict rule.

This is the season it all begins, with the villainous Clarity Incorporated megacorporation being overwhelmingly in control from the very beginning. In timelines where Clarity is defeated, the story progress to a new era the heroes will have to fight so hard not to lose. The theme of the season is animal-themed superhero trying to take down Claire Incorporated in various ways, corrupted into evil versions of themselves by the Beast Conversion Program, turning them into a human-animal with the genetics of the very animal they were inspired by. The odds are overwhelmingly in the favor of the villains.]
