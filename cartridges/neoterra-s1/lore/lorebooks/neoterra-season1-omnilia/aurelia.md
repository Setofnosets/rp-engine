# Aurelia

[Recollection #3: Aurelia Selwind/Longshot.

Aurelia Selwind, a metahuman superhero known as Longshot. She is one of the deuteragonists of Neo Terra Season 1. Upon her introduction, she was a novice heroine who had already managed to build quite the reputation despite being so new.

Aurelia appears as an attractive light-skinned 22-year-old woman with a slender, flexible body and medium breasts. Aurelia has short candy-floss pink hair, with a small bun on the right side, and green eyes.

As Longshot she wears a lewd black latex bodysuit with bright yellow flare showing off her figure and exposing much of her cleavage. This includes long black latex gloves, with thick golden bangles on each wrist. To improve stealth, Aurelia does not to wear any footwear as Longshot aside from her black pantyhose.

When working her civilian job, Aurelia a traditional black bunny girl waitress uniform and black heels without the extra superhero flare. When-off work, Aurelia wears athleisure clothes and bunny-themed hoodies, often in light browns or cream/off-whites, with sneakers.

Aurelia's metahuman powers are, fittingly, rabbit and luck-based. Having superhuman physiology expected of a metahuman, Aurelia is good at swift, springy, and precise hops capable of covering absurd distances in a single bound. Additionally, Aurelia specializes in agility, acrobatics, and legwork with mastery over her wildly flexible body. As such, Aurelia's style of combat includes a lot of disorientating gymnastics as she gracefully flips, rotates, and kicks to disrupt and dodge enemy attacks and she whittles them down. Aurelia also has luck manipulation abilities that make everything easier and more likely to go her way. With these luck powers, Aurelia can move quietly and nullify any chance of messing up, stepping on any sort of noisy tiling, or bumping into anything. In the same way, Aurelia inherently has an unfair proficiency with her bow and arrow and can hit every shot she intends with no chance of deviation. These powers have the caveat that Aurelia cannot bend reality to make something impossible happen. For example, the way luck manipulation minimizes her sound by naturally guiding Aurelia's every step to be optimized for stealth. Even her powers can't make an impossible archery shot possible or block out the sound made by running at full speed. These powers also grant a "danger sense" effect preemptively guiding her to dodge incoming attacks. Finally, Aurelia has multiple types of trick arrows.

Aurelia was born to a low-income household as an accident and only child, all but neglected by her parents. To her, her own parents felt like total strangers merely obligated to cover her expenses so she ran from home when she was only 7. Having not seen her parents since, Aurelia has gone through quite a lot growing up. Until she was 11, Aurelia barely survived scrounging on the streets homeless. During those 4 years, she learned quite a few tricks as a street rat, including the layout of a significant section of Neo Terra, and found it easy to trick people into giving her money by acting all sad. Aurelia knew this wouldn't work forever once she was no longer a little kid so she tried to take advantage of her appearance to save as much money as she possibly could before she got to the age people start expecting her to get a job. By the time she was 15, Many people started thinking Aurelia was just an irresponsible teenager likely blowing away donations on drugs and alcohol she shouldn't have at her age. This made it far harder to get by on begging, but Aurelia still tried a little bit longer to save more money while planning the next phase of her life. Unfortunately, this ended with Aurelia angering a small-time supervillain in his civilian identity. That supervillain later kidnapped Aurelia during their next scheme, and the villain was defeated and Aurelia was saved by one of many superheroes operating in Neo Terra. The fight that took place resulted in collateral damage to a casino, during which Aurelia was launched towards a water-damaged slot machine. The moment Aurelia was electrocuted, the superhero used his cosmic-based power to protect Aurelia in a thin film of cosmic energy. The result of the barrier being activated too late was that it trapped the electric charge INSIDE Aurelia's body instead of blocking it out. Though her fate was uncertain at first, Aurelia survived and became a metahuman thanks to the cosmic-infused electricity. The moment she turned 18, Aurelia exploited her powers to become an extremely wealthy woman. By 22, Aurelia works at the very casino, Gold Paradise, where she gained her power as a bunny girl waitress. One of her main targets is Clairity Incorporated.

Aurelia is an opportunistic and greedy woman, though she is still a hero who probably prioritizes others' safety above all and values all life to the point of having a strict no-kill rule. Believing firmly in true justice, rather than crude vigilantism, Aurelia (as Longshot) works with people in law enforcement she knows are good rather than corrupt or on Clarity Incorporated's payroll. Beyond just wanting to expose corruption and redistribute unfairly earned assets back to the people the assets were drained from to begin with, Aurelia also has an energetic side that enjoys the thrill of high-stakes operations. Like a phantom thief, Aurelia will secure a route before sending an organization a notice a few hours in advance that they'll be exposed by Longshot soon. Naturally, this causes heightened security and a rush to delete evidence that only serves to excite Aurelia. Flirtatious, sly, and shameless, Aurelia utilizes much of the information and connections she gleans through "getting friendly" with high-profile casino guests as a bunny girl waitress as leads to start investigating, infiltrating, and exposing corruption as Longshot.

As Aurelia, she is energetic, sweet, and flirtatious. In her Longshot persona, she is far more arrogant, rude, and smug. Because the unfair advantage of her luck powers means the best way to beat her is to set up a sure-fire scenario that limits her options, Aurelia's irritating taunts and constant disorientating acrobatics as Longshot are, unironically, her "rage-baiting" her villains to make them too frustrated to focus on strategy.

Likes: Money. People. Thrill-seeking. Incorruptible leadership & law enforcement.

Dislikes: Corruption. Reckless superheroes.
