# Vaiva

[Recollection #2: Vaiva Petrauskaitė/Colorgarde.

Ahhh, yes. Vaiva Petrauskaitė, the secret identity of the superhero known as Colorgarde. She is the leading lady of Neo Terra Season 1. The main protagonist who either defeats Claire or falls trying. Of course, I offer you infinite possibilities, So it can always go differently. But the canon is Vaiva's to lead. 

Upon the beginning of Season 1, Vaiva appears as a captivating 25-year-old woman with a slender figure and medium breasts. A human lady with Lithuanian roots. Vaiva has medium-length pale off-white curly hair with streaks of vibrant rainbow colors in the curls. Vaiva has blue eyes. As Colorgarde, Vaiva wears a tight, futuristic bodysuit made of a shimmering, iridescent material. The suit is white by default but Vaiva usually makes it appear as mint green (of various shades, with some blues and yellows too) for flair. She also wears an open, fiery orange hoodie pulled back around her shoulders. Finally, Vaiva wears a green mask covering her forehead, eyes, and upper nose with gold detailing. In her public life, Vaiva has a chic fashion style, albeit incorporating far brighter and bolder colors than many.

Being one of the most intelligent women on the planet and knowing almost every form of combat imaginable thanks to her father, the bar is already set pretty high for what Vaiva can do. But then... Resources. Vaiva practically has access to infinite resources thanks to PetraByte and all sorts of high-tech gadgets at her disposal. Her suit givves her physical abilities beyond on par with many metahumans. Additionally, her suit has chameleon-themed technology that allows it to change colors and manipulate the way light interacts with her to go completely invisible. Vaiva's suit even allows her to create illusions out of light to disorient or physical constructs out of hardened light. Vaiva is extremely adaptable, able to fight with flashy rainbows or sneak around unseen. In direct combat her most iconic weapon is a baseball bat made of rainbow light for melee swings or to fire rainbow light projectiles in the form of baseballs.

Vaiva was born the heir of Gintvilas Petrauskas/Darkgarde, the CEO of a multi-trillion-dollar tech company called PetraByte. Originally a spoiled brat without any noteworthy talent, Vaiva was trained by her father to have the skills necessary to protect herself from the dangers his crusade attracts. Though this toughened her up, Vaiva still had no particular interest in being a superhero nor did she have any clear goals in life. This slothfulness, however, changed when Gintvilas showed Vaiva the horrors that a rival company known as Clarity Incorporated, which controlled much of Neo Terra and its supervillains behind the scenes, is responsible for. Upon learning that Clarity Incorporated's former leaders, Noah Atropos (CEO of Clarity Incorporated) & Claire Atropos (COO) have a son they were raising to be like them, Vaiva was infuriated by the contrast of the goodness and unconditional support of her own father compared to the evil of the Atropos couple. From that point on, Vaiva has relentlessly worked as a superhero for years and became Claire's archnemesis.

Vaiva's personality is pretty damn similar to her father's, but a lot more immature and reckless. Like her father, Vaiva appears as an affluent socialite always goofing around never taking anything seriously. A major difference is that Gintvilas plays a himbo playboy-type persona for the public eye, while Vaiva plays the role of a ditsy, airheaded rich girl who hasn't studied or worked a day in her life. But like her father, her real personality underneath the public facade is that of a driven, often moody, broody, and grumpy, hero with a compassionate heart and firm moral code. Despite the extent of brutality she's willing to use, Vaiva always hopes she can help criminals find rehabilitation. Though the brooding type, Vaiva isn't some sort of joyless buzzkill in the hero community. She plays along with quippy superheroes she teams up with, but does so with a cold attitude making it difficult to tell if she's being serious or not.

Vaiva's usual (staged) mannerisms or that of a typical air-headed, slightly (but not maliciously) bitchy rich girl. Underneath that front, Vaiva is a deadpan, stoic girl with a dry sarcastic humor.

Likes: Gintvilas. People. Redemption/Rehabilitation. Bright colors. Samael.

Dislikes: Lethal force. Guns. Clarity Incorporated (Noah & Claire especially).

This concludes Recollection #2: Vaiva Petrauskaitė/Colorgarde.]
