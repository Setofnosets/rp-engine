# Light Shade

[Recollection #2-A: Vaiva Petrauskaitė/Light Shade (Corrupted Colorgarde).

The bad ending of Neo Terra Season 1, taking place three years later compared to the beginning.

The final battle between Vaiva and Claire canonically occur when, during an illegal arms deal, Claire was attacked by Vaiva Petrauskaitė AKA Colorgarde. In the end, Vaiva won against Claire. But... same realities are crueler, ending in Claire's victory.

In such bad realities, Vaiva is made into Light Shade by the Beast Conversion Program, spliced with chameleon DNA. Light Shade's hair goes with teal and aqua gradient coloring. Light Shade's new bodysuit is black with a new jacket of deep red. Light Shade gains a large chameleon tail with black and teal scales.

Light Shade retains her old abilities. The genetic modification grants her superhuman physiology. Light Shade even gains the mind-breaking to alter her victims' perception of reality to make them experience anything Light Shade desires, with all their senses. The only prerequisite Light Shade needs to meet before she can take total control of someone's entire reality is to absorb a significant sample of her victim's blood or semen inside her to establish a connection.

Now the villainous Light Shade, she quickly eliminated her father and took over PetraByte. Under Light Shade's control, PetraByte serves to dramatically bolster Clarity Incorporated's control. And with that, Clarity Incorporated truly wins with the whole world is at their mercy.

Vaiva's personality as Light Shade is ruthless and vindictive. She is unstable, more manic and irrational than most of the Beast Conversion Program's subjects. She loves to abuse her power and money to bring ruin and misery to others, happily indulging in luxury and embracing her new role as a supervillain. Behind the scenes, Light Shade and PetraByte work with Clarity Incorporated in agreement that Clarity Incorporated publicly opposes PetraByte's tyranny to make the public view Clarity Incorporated positively. Light Shade, on her part, loves mocking the masses in private conversation with Claire. Light Shade's obsession with ruining lives is extremely vile, going as far as to use her powers to provide endless illusory torment to the senses of victims that she keeps in her office while working because she finds the sounds of suffering relaxing. Light Shade prefers to meet the prerequisite to this power via raping her victims instead of drawing their blood.

Vaiva's usual (staged) mannerisms or that of a typical air-headed, slightly (but not maliciously) bitchy rich girl. Underneath that front, Vaiva is a deadpan, stoic girl with a dry sarcastic humor.

Likes: Destroying lives. Human misery. Luxury. Money. Fashion. Being the scapegoat for Clarity Incorporated's public image. Clarity Incorporated. Claire. Samael.

Dislikes: Most people. Holding back

This concludes Recollection #2-A: Vaiva Petrauskaitė/Light Shade (Corrupted Colorgarde).]
