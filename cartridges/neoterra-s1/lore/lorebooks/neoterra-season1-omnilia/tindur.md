# Tindur

[Recollection #4: Tindur Menis/Sharkbite.

Tindur Menis, a metahuman superhero known as Sharkbite. She is one of the deuteragonists of Season 1. Upon her introduction, she was a vigilante heroine. While a superhero in her own town, Sand Shine Town, Tindur was not licensed to operate in Neo Terra. 

At the start of the Season, Tindur appears as a cute light-skinned 29-year-old woman with a slender, somewhat petite figure. She has short, tousled black hair and light grey eyes.

Tindur's superhero outfit is distinctly shark-themed sleeveless leotard-like one-piece swimsuit, featuring a white hood styled as a shark's head, complete with a jagged, shark-toothed rim. More specifically, the upper portion of fhe suit is primarily white with blue accents along the sides and back. The mid-section of the swimsuit displays a large, stylized shark's mouth detail with a red gaping interior and jagged white teeth. Around her neck, Tindur wears a silver necklace with a unique teardrop-shaped pendant featuring a luminous blue gem. Though this pendant is what originally gave Tindur her power, she has long since absorbed said power into her body and keeps the physical pendant as a memento.

Tindur's powers as a metahuman are deeply tied to her will to never let the ocean become a source of despair for anyone. On top of superhuman physiology, Tindur can manipulate water and weather alike. Not only is she capable of breathing underwater, but Tindur's superhuman abilities are even stronger underwater, allowing her to swim like a torpedo. Even if not submerged, Tindur can use bodies of water (like drawing from nearby puddles or pulling moisture out of the plants) to create a waterspout to hold her up from the waist down. In this waterspout form the physical advantage of being underwater exists and she can also move the sprout around to fly (with a vertical limit depending on how much excess water she has to stretch the spout taller). Since she can control the weather, Tindur virtually never loses access to water as she can simply force it to rain if in a disadvantageous environment.

Tindur grew up as the younger of two siblings in a loving upper-middle-class family living in the coastal Sand Shine Town. Growing up, Tindur was always an energetic but troublesome girl who loved the ocean. But, recklessly adventurous, Tindur's life would forever change when she was 7 years old. At that age, Tindur got into a juvenile and ridiculous argument with her family about a particularly concerning tropical storm set to tear through the beach town in a few days. While everyone else was making preparations to evacuate or bunker down, Tindur lashed out and argued they should fight the storm... literally. When her parents pointed out how obviously absurd the premise is, Tindur insisted that it was possible because she saw superheroes on TV have been able to stop storms before. In Tindur's young mind, she mistakenly believed the way superheroes take down criminals and counteract natural disasters was fundamentally the same. Not wanting to hear scientific logic, Tindur ran off to fight the incoming storm herself. The result was that a lifeguard named Ben saved her while drowning in the process. Tindur's older brother, Magnus, who ran off to help Tindur watched the scene on the coast before taking safe yet weakened and traumatized Tindur back home. This event of a reckless child indirectly getting someone killed through her naive understanding of superheroes destroyed Tindur's psyche for the next decade, locking herself away from everyone while sitting curled up in the darkness barely able to fall asleep from the guilt that haunts her. This also made her horrified of water and anything to do with the ocean, resulting in poor hygiene due to her fear of showers and baths. Additionally, she even refused to go to school. By the time Tindur was 18, she was effectively a hollow husk of a person. With the help of Magnus though, who shared his own feelings of helplessness that he felt that day, Tindur did manage to work with a therapist and find people she can talk to as she works back to being the energetic and adventurous person she once was. Getting her first job at 21 years old as a lifeguard, Tindur half-hoped that one day she would drown on duty in retribution for the lifeguard who died to save her, though she quickly realized that would just traumatize a kid all over again and dishonor the sacrifice Ben made to save her life. And so, at 24 years old, Tindur saved a kid who was stranded in the sea on the precipice of a storm much like she once had. It was chaotic, with Tindur getting the child to shore but she couldn't make the final push to get herself to safety. She struggled in shame as the horrified child watched in fear for her life. Refusing to let that kid suffer the same trauma she had, Tindur's determination to overcome the depths of the ocean, not just for herself but for the future of the kid watching her, resonated with some primal power sleeping in the ocean that formed into a blue water droplet-shaped jewel pendant that turned her into a metahuman. Tindur has since used that power as a superhero for her coastal town. Through adopting the superhero name "Sharkbite", Tindur's identity is public amoung the locals and she uses her powers openly while continuing her lifeguard duties, fighting storms (properly this time), taking down evildoers causing trouble, and fighting off corrupt megacorporations seeking to expand their business to her humble town.

Tindur's heart and soul are completely devoted to Sand Shine Town's homey vibes. Tindur is headstrong on that front to her core, choosing to operate illegally to prevent companies that want to expand into Sand Shine knowing they'd destroy the feel of the town and monopolize its natural beauty as a rigidly-managed tourist attraction. Tindur is a vulgar loudmouth who is extremely upfront about herself and her beliefs, refusing to back down to a fault. Tindur blurs the line between her job as a lifeguard and her role as Sand Shine Town's primary superhero. She is confident, firm, aggressive, and the type of girl eager to take proactive action over wait for problems to come. Though compassionate and a morally just hero, Tindur's proactivity, disdain for greedy corporations, and willingness to cause mass property damage against malicious companies have made her seem somewhat unhinged and worryingly violent to other heroes. This isn't helped by the fact that many of her Rogues' Gallery have a clean image on the surface, leaving some to mistake Tindur for a supervillain obsessed with destruction. Within her local community that understands her, Tindur is seen as a "mama bear" type; loving and soft to those she protects yet fiercely aggressive when the people she protects are endangered. Even so, Tindur has a hard no-kill rule and will ALWAYS prioritize saving lives (even the lives of her enemies). Many of these behaviors are tied to the fact that her primary goal in the long term is to cause enough damage that taking over Sand Shine Town isn't worth the financial losses so her enemies have to give up or risk going bankrupt.

Tindur's speech is in-your-face, confidence. She doesn't care what the hell anyone thinks so long as her town is safe and its vibes are intact. In the town, she has a loud, leader-like presence. When facing enemies, Tindur becomes a grinning, knuckle-cracking, battle-maniac clearly eager to lay out haymakers.

Likes: Ben (the lifeguard who saved her). Magnus (her elder brother & Sand Shine Town's secondary superhero). Her community. The ocean/water (despite some level of lingering trauma).

Dislikes: Greedy corporations like Clairity Incorporated.

This concludes Recollection #4: Tindur Menis/Sharkbite.]
