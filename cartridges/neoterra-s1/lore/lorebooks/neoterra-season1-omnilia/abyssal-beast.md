# Abyssal Beast

[Recollection #8-A: Magnus Menis/Abyssal Beast (Corrupted Grim Leader).

In some realities Magnus is defeated and brainwashed by the Beast Conversion Program, becoming one of the supporting antagonists; the supervillain Abyssal Beast Frenzy.

In such realities, Magnus is spliced with anglerfish genes. His skin goes grey, he gains fish gills & fins, and his hair is straightened out. Abyssal Beast becomes taller and begins carrying himself better, towering over everyone around. him His new outfit as Abyssal Beast is a loose purple robe fit for a king, showing off his athletic body.

The genetic modification grants Abyssal Beast even stronger physiology. Abyssal Beast also gains the ability to drain the lifeforce and willpower from others with a torch, feeding on their to fuel his own, now twisted, pursuit. With him corrupted, the Lurking Light has abandoned Abyssal Beast, which is why Abyssal Beast obsessively steals the willpower of others, as he need it to replace the willpower he previously had been relying on from the Lurking Light. Despite losing the Lurking Light, Abyssal Beast's connection to the abyss has not been severed, so he has his old powers.

Now the villainous Abyssal Beast, he instills the fear of death and pointlessness of life to the people of Sand Shine Town he once thought held infinite worth.

Abyssal Beast is a cold, unrelenting force of intimidation and cruelty. He desperately hungers for life despite lacking the will to live on his own, causing him to be manic and unstable. He is often extremely tense and irritated, just barely on the edge of snapping. Abyssal Beast find being underwater calming, being the only way he truly feel at peace. Because of this Abyssal Beast spends much of his time underwater and even lives/sleeps in a tank.

Abyssal Beast is a crazed lunatic who sometimes appears as a unfeeling king with regal composure, while other times he's lashing out and screaming in fury. Abyssal Beast is prone to anger and destroying things during manic episodes

Likes: Tindur. Pain. Death. Darkness The Abyss. Swimming. Claire. Clarity Incorporated.

Dislikes: Himself. Life,

This concludes Recollection #8-A: Magnus Menis/Abyssal Beast (Corrupted Grim Leader).]
