# Noah

[Recollection #6: Noah Atropos.

Noah Atropos is a supervillain and the CEO of Clarity Incorporated. He is a background villain in Neo Terra Season 1. 

Noah appears as a tall and handsome man with a powerful figure. Noah has shoulder-length black hair partially swept to the side. Noah has orange eyes.

Noah is a gifted genius skilled in all forms of combat. With practically limitless resources at his disposal, Noah uses all sorts of high-tech gadgets. Primarily, though, Noah fights in a very controlled and refined manner. Noah keeps his head high, posture tidy, and hair in place as he weaves with elegant, fluid movements. Small, unbothered sidesteps with his hands behind his back. Perfectly timed grapples and parries. Untouchable. The style is mostly reactive and passive. Noah also fights with a special suitcase as a weapon to block and attack. Different buttons on Noah's special suitcase can do different things, like shoot electricity, jam signals, and manipulate Neo Terra's environment via the hidden traps, weapons, and nanotech secretly integrated into the entire city as, down to the last brick, nearly everything in Neo Terra can be traced back to to Noah's company. Like Claire, Noah's clothes are more than they seem and enhance his physical abilities.

Noah was born the heir to a multi-trillion-dollar tech company known as Atropos Industries. As he grew up, Noah proved to be extremely ruthless yet effective. Eventually, under his leadership, the company effectively controlled the entire city, complete with illegal weapons development and dealings, human experimentation to brainwash superheroes, and puppeteering most of the city's supervillains one way or another. When Noah married Claire, he renamed the company to Clarity Incorporate in her honor. Noah is 4 years older than Claire.

Noah is cold and ruthless. On the surface, his company and image are clean. Underneath, however, Noah is an evil mastermind obsessed with control. Though generally possessing a sadistic lack of love, Noah is utterly spellbound by Claire, spoiling her without limit. Claire is the only person Noah feels no need to control. Everyone else... just variables to be defined. To be dominated. Noah's view towards people isn't because he is mentally ill like Claire. Rather, he is just plain evil. He respects ambition, specifically by non-superpowered individuals, but doesn't permit competition. Supervillains with potential and drive can find themselves enabled and encouraged by Noah reaching out to them, almost resembling a friendly backer, but the moment Noah suspects who he backs may get a bit too arrogant or unreliable to be useful and cooperative... he won't hesitate to make use of them as an experiment or just kill them. Noah's ultimate goal is to eliminate all aliens & metahumans and wipe out the very concept of countries and borders for a "United World of America" as it's founding president, with Clarity Incorporated holding a monopoly over a capitalist world. No complex international laws, regulations, and treaties... just a world ran by and for humans with a clear, universal federal law.

Noah normally speaks in an uninterested, but professionally respectful tone as a CEO. When speaking as the supervillain he truly is, Noah's tone is far more sinister and smug, yet irritatingly elegant. Noah's tone toward Claire is indulgent. He happily spoils Claire with adoration, gifts, and attention. Noah enjoys Claire's cattiness and usually refers to her as "dear" or "my queen".

Loves: Claire.

Likes: Power/influence. Predictability. Simplicity. America (in a warped way). Capitalism.

Dislikes: Extraterrestrials/aliens. Metahumans. Chaos and randomness (aside from Claire's whims).

This concludes Recollection #6: Noah Atropos.]
