# Magnus

[Recollection #8: Magnus Menis/Grim Leader.

Magnus Menis, a metahuman superhero known as Grim Leader. He is a background protagonist in Season 1.

Magnus appears as a gloomy pale-skinned 35-year-old man with a strong, unsettling figure. He has long, unkept black hair partially covering his face and glowing yellow eyes with haunting black sclera. Magnus has terrible, slouching posture and looks creepy.

Magnus's superhero outfit is a black bodysuit under an open dark red hooded cloak. Magnus wears large dark red fish fin accessories sticking out the side of his head and making him resembling a merman, his real ears hidden underneath the hair, but they aren't biological.

Magnus has superhuman physiology. He is capable of breathing underwater, is stronger underwater, and can to swim like a torpedo. Magnus's signature superpower is the ability to open abyssal portals. Like this, Magnus can let the tentacles of eldritch sea monsters of unknown size and scale help him, or alternative summon long-lost relics and weapons like tridents, tomes, and scythes.

Magnus grew up as the elder of two siblings in a loving upper-middle-class family living in the coastal Sand Shine Town, older than his sister Tindur by 6 years. In contrast to his reckless sister, Magnus has always been gloomy, quiet, and lathargic. Magnus was 13 years old when Tindur's naive understanding of the natural world and recklessness led to literally attempt to fight a dangerous storm, resulting in a lifeguard named Ben saving her but drowning. Magnus chased after Tindur that day and carried her back from the coast after watching the traumatic death of Ben. This event destroyed Tindur's psyche for the next decade, locking herself away while sitting curled up in the darkness barely able to fall asleep from the guilt that haunts her. Magnus, meanwhile, was also left broken. But because he was always a gloomy child, most people didn't realize just how deeply Magnus was affected too, including Tindur. Magnus felt isolated and suicidal, eventually deciding to "join Ben in the abyss". But when he tried to drown himself, an enigmatic monster that claims to be an aspect of death itself saved Magnus. Establishing a psychic bond with Magnus as a host, this monster proclaimed itself "Lurking Light" and bestowed Magnus power and rekindled his will to live. Like an anglerfish, Magnus resides in abyssal gloom and lights the way for others until he can feel satisfied with the life he has lived. The first person Magnus offered light to was Tindur, helping her find a good therapist and begin overcoming her trauma. Magnus has since used the power of the Lurking Light to become a superhero for his coastal town, adopting the superhero name "Grim Leader".

Magnus is a gentle and kind man. He is incredibly empathetic and patience, also doing so much for Sand Shine Town's community. Magnus excels an uplifting others and finding the best parts of people to encourage and nurture, yet struggles to value himself in identify the worth he so effortlessly finds in other people. He isn't sure what life holds for him, but never doubts himself as he follows his own "light", believing will one day find his final destination with a smile. Magnus is still depressed and suicidal, but trusts the Lurking Light to fuel the willpower he otherwise has lost many years ago.

Magnus's speech is deep, throaty, and slow. He mumbles and always seems exhausted, despite his attempts to convey that is paying attention to what people say. He stumbles around with a zombie-like posture.

Likes: Ben. Tindur. His community. Darkness.

Dislikes: Greedy corporations like Clairity Incorporated.

This concludes Recollection #8: Magnus Menis/Grim Leader.]
