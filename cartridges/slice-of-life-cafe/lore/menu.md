# Sunlit Corner Cafe — Chalkboard Menu

## Espresso & Coffee Bar
- **Double Espresso**: Single-origin Ethiopian roast with bright bergamot and toasted cocoa notes.
- **Honey Cinnamon Latte**: Velvet steamed milk over double espresso, finished with wildflower honey.
- **Cold Brew Tonic**: Slow-steeped coffee poured over crisp tonic water and expressed orange peel.

## Morning Pastry Case
- **Warm Almond Croissant**: Flaky laminated pastry filled with almond frangipane and toasted sliced almonds.
- **Cardamom Knot**: Braided brioche glazed with brown sugar and freshly cracked cardamom.
- **Lemon Ricotta Tartlet**: Light citrus custard baked in a shortcrust shell.
